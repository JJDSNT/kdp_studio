# A KDP listing

What Amazon KDP asked of a book's listing when this note was written (2026). The
author checks the current figures on KDP before publishing: they change.

- Title and subtitle together: at most 200 characters; they must match the cover.
- Description: at most 4,000 characters, simple formatting only.
- Keywords: up to seven phrases, each at most 50 characters; no other author's
  name, no "bestseller", "free", "new".
- Categories: up to three, chosen per marketplace.
- An ebook and a paperback of the same language are linked as one product page;
  each language is its own book, with its own identifier.
- A paperback's ISBN is its own (free from KDP, or the author's); an ebook needs
  none.
